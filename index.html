How it works

Simple. Transparent. Accountable.

GramSeva bridges citizens and Panchayat officials with technology.

📱

File in Minutes

Choose a category, describe your issue, add a photo and GPS location — submit in under 2 minutes.

🔍

Track in Real Time

Every complaint gets a unique ID like GRM-2026-000123. Track from Submitted to Verified.

✅

Verify Resolution

You confirm when your issue is truly fixed. Officials upload proof; you approve or escalate.

🎙️

Voice Input (Marathi)

Speak your complaint in Marathi. AI transcribes and auto-classifies it — no typing needed.

🗺️

Village Problem Map

All complaints pinned on your village map. See hotspots; officials prioritise faster.

🤖

AI Smart Routing

Auto category detection, priority scoring and duplicate detection — zero manual sorting.
